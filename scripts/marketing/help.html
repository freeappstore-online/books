Reading, listening and offline help

Is Books free, and do I need an account?

Books is free to use. Open a book in your browser without creating an account or entering payment details.

Does every book have an audiobook?

All catalogue titles can be read. Only titles marked Audio have narration. Alice's Adventures in Wonderland is the first fully narrated book; the With audio filter shows the selected narrated titles.

How do I read or listen offline?

Open a book in the app while connected. On a phone, open More reading options (the three dots), then choose Save offline. Reading-only books save their text; narrated books also download their available audio chapters. Wait for Saved offline before disconnecting. Manage downloads and see their size in My books.

Where are my progress, notes and downloads stored?

They stay in this browser on this device. They do not automatically sync to another device or browser. Clearing site data removes them. Browsers may also remove downloaded files under storage pressure; reconnect and update an incomplete offline copy to repair it.

Questions or a playback problem?

Check the Books issue tracker or report a problem there. Include your device, browser, book and chapter so the problem can be reproduced.
